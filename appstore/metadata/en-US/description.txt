I Glasset ("In the Glass") is blind tasting with friends – no account, no server, and no way to cheat.

THE HOST BUILDS THE EVENING
Create the tasting, choose the number of glasses and enter each bottle with grape, country, region, vintage, alcohol and price. Photograph every bottle. Guests see nothing until you reveal.

EVERYONE JOINS WITH A CODE
The phones in the room find the host over your own Wi-Fi. Type the six-character code and everyone lands in the same lobby. Nobody has to sign up for anything.

TASTE BLIND, GUESS AND SCORE
Every glass gets a score from 1 to 10 and a guess: aroma, taste, grape, price, alcohol, vintage, country and region – plus the extraordinary, when the host has flagged something special about the glass. Up to 26 points in play per glass.

THE REVEAL LANDS ON EVERY PHONE
When the host reveals, the bottle photo appears on every screen together with the group average, your own score and your guessing points, category by category.

THE CLUB KEEPS SCORE
Create a group for your wine club. Standings accumulate across every evening, the club can see what it has spent per bottle, who guesses prices best, and which bottles set records.

YOUR OWN ARCHIVE
Every glass you have tasted is kept on your phone with photo, score and notes. Browse your top tastings and get a taste profile built from what you actually rated highly.

THE BLIND CANNOT BE BROKEN
Until a glass is revealed, the host's phone sends the others only the glass number – never the name, price or photo. Points are awarded by the host's phone from the host's own answer key.

NO ACCOUNT. NO SERVER. NO DATA COLLECTED.
Everything stays on your phones. Tastings are shared directly between the devices on the local network, and past tastings open with no network at all.

The app is in Danish. It was made for wine, but works just as well for whisky, beer, rum and coffee – and anything else that ends up in a glass.
